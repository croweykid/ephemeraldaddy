| Period / Year            | What Changed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2nd c. CE**            | Ptolemy writes both *Almagest* astronomy and *Tetrabiblos* astrology. They are distinct genres, but not separated disciplines. Same intellectual ecosystem.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **1200s-1500s**          | Astrology is embedded in university medicine, astronomy, natural philosophy, and court practice. Not fringe.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **1543**                 | Copernicus publishes heliocentric astronomy. Important for astronomy, but not an immediate astrology/astronomy breakup.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **1609-1619**            | Kepler formulates planetary laws while still practicing/reforming astrology. So even here, no clean split.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **1660 / 1666**          | Royal Society and French Académie des Sciences mark the rise of new scientific institutions. Astronomy gets institutionalized as mathematics/physics; astrology is increasingly outside the room.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **1687**                 | Newton’s *Principia* gives astronomy a mechanical physics foundation. This is probably the best symbolic “break year.”                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **1700s**                | Astrology is largely pushed out of respectable academic natural knowledge. Cambridge summarizes this as astrology disappearing from legitimate natural knowledge during the **17th and 18th centuries** [Cambridge History of Science](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-science/astrology/17E3D5BB41AE55616C6B9AB7949FE0F1). |
| **By late 1700s / 1800** | The modern distinction is basically in place: astronomy = science of celestial bodies; astrology = divinatory/symbolic practice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
