| Dimension                    | What It Measures                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Harm severity**            | Death, torture, sexual violence, imprisonment, dispossession, psychological destruction, scale, duration.                |
| **Intent**                   | Accident, negligence, recklessness, instrumental cruelty, sadism, ideological extermination.                             |
| **Agency / constraint**      | How much choice they realistically had: coercion, survival pressure, age, dependency, institutional threat.              |
| **Foreseeability**           | Did they know what would happen? Did people warn them? Did evidence accumulate?                                          |
| **Repetition**               | One catastrophic act vs repeated pattern after chances to stop.                                                          |
| **Vulnerability of victims** | Children, captives, civilians, dependents, patients, subordinates, occupied populations.                                 |
| **Power asymmetry**          | How much control they had over victims’ ability to escape or resist.                                                     |
| **Opportunity / means**      | How much capacity they had to enact harm.                                                                                |
| **Latent willingness**       | What they seemed willing to do if given power, even if they lacked the means.                                            |
| **Remorse / repair**         | Denial, pride, minimization, apology, restitution, prevention of further harm.                                           |
| **Wasted moral capacity**    | Education, stability, affection, resources, alternatives, social support. Basically: how available was “not doing that”? |
| **Evidence confidence**      | Public record quality: conviction, confession, archives, credible witnesses, rumor, propaganda, contested allegations.   |
